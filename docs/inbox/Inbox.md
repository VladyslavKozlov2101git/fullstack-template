# inbox

Drop raw incoming material here: a brief, a ticket export, a call transcript, a client PDF.

The `ingest` skill processes it — a note into `sources/` from [[Source|99-Templates/Source]], the
affected pages updated, a [[Journal]] entry, and the original filed under `sources/raw/YYYY-MM-DD/`.
Nothing should stay here after a session: an empty inbox is the signal that everything has been
read.

A feature brief goes through `/feature spec` as well, which writes `specs/<feature>/request.md` —
the inbox note is the provenance, the spec is the agreement.
